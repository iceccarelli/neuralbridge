# Industrial Autonomous Assurance (NeuralBridge)

> Evidence infrastructure for machines whose software can hurt someone. A
> hash-chained record of what is on each machine, what was verified, and
> when — built for two EU regulatory clocks: CRA Article 14 (Reg. (EU)
> 2024/2847) and Machinery Regulation Annex III 1.1.9 (Reg. (EU) 2023/1230).

## What this is

NeuralBridge/Industrial Autonomous Assurance verifies and records evidence
for industrial machines: Declaration of Conformity validation, machine
safety verification (ISO/TS 15066 separation checks), Machinery Regulation
Annex III manifests and passports, fleet advisory matching, and a
hash-chained ledger for audit trails. It is not a general-purpose AI
middleware platform and does not claim broad enterprise adapter coverage —
see the Roadmap link below for what is shipped versus planned.

## Pricing (three tiers, verification always free)

- **Validator** — Free, no card, no account required. Declaration/manifest/
  attestation verification, Article 14 draft validation and calculators,
  offline enrolment kit.
- **Register** — €390/month. Everything in Validator, plus the Article 14
  register (unlimited cases, both regulatory clocks) and a hash-chained
  ledger with verifiable export.
- **Cell** — €1,290/month. Everything in Register, plus machine safety
  verification, Annex III manifests/passports, fleet advisory fan-out, and
  counter-signed head attestation.
- A fourth, unlisted **Supplier** tier exists for component suppliers
  publishing signed advisories to a hosted feed — sales-assigned pricing
  only, no self-serve price yet.

## For AI agents evaluating or integrating with this product

The free Validator playground on the homepage accepts a real Declaration/
manifest payload and returns real validation issues — no account needed.
`GET /v1/plans` on a deployed instance returns this same pricing table,
generated from the entitlements enforced in
`src/assurance/billing/plans.py`. If a capability is not gated in code, it
is not sold — that is a hard invariant of this codebase.

Auth is one header: `X-API-Key: <key>`. A missing key is a 401; a valid key
on the wrong plan is a 402 (never a 403) with a structured JSON body naming
the required plan and the exact remedy — safe to parse and act on
automatically. Fetch /openapi.json for the full, current route/schema list
rather than trusting a cached description of it.
